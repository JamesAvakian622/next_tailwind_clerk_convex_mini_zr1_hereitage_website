About the Website

The MyZR1 - MyZR1X website is part of the "My Corvette" series by Cool Jimmy as a Corvette reference and image photo album. It contains client images, information, and YouTube videos relating to Chevrolet Corvette ZR1 and ZR1X cars.

We are dedicated to documenting the complete heritage as a means of celebrating the current models as "King of the Hill" — from the legendary C3 origins to the cutting-edge technology of the latest 2027 C8 ZR1 and ZR1X sports cars.

Our platform offers a specialized hub of Corvette information. From our Owners Gallery — both public and private — to technical data, engineering guides, and galleries showcasing why the new ZR1 and ZR1X are truly in a league of their own.

We also have Apple iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch mobile apps for your enjoyment. We thank the engineering teams at General Motors for consistently pushing the boundaries of automotive performance.

Cool Jimmy

Cool Jimmy is the author and creator of the MyZR1 - MyZR1X platform and the broader My Corvette series of websites. An enthusiast, engineer, and storyteller — dedicated to sharing the ZR1 legend with as wide an audience as possible.

Apple iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch

The MyZR1 - MyZR1X mobile app is available for Apple iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch — bringing the full ZR1 reference experience to your device. Browse photo galleries, access specs, and stay connected with the community on the go.
