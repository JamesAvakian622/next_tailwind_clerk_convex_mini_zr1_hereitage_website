Service Reminder System

The ZR1's Oil Life Monitor (OLM) uses an algorithm analyzing RPM cycles, engine temperature, and driving conditions to calculate remaining oil life as a percentage. When it drops below 10%, a message appears in the Driver Information Center.

Separate alerts appear for tire rotation, DCT filter, brake fluid inspection, and coolant check at their respective intervals. All can be acknowledged and reset through the DIC menu after service is performed.

Track-mode drive sessions accelerate the OLM algorithm — expect shorter intervals when the car is regularly taken to circuit events. The OLM does not account for brake fluid degradation under track heat, so manual brake fluid monitoring is recommended for track drivers.
