DCT Filter Service

7,500-Mile DCT Filter — Critical First Service

The Tremec 8-speed DCT uses a serviceable filter cartridge that must be replaced at 7,500 miles for the first service, then every 15,000 miles thereafter. This is more frequent than a traditional automatic transmission because the dual-clutch pack generates fine metallic particles during the break-in period.

Missing the first DCT filter service is the single most common maintenance mistake on C8 ZR1 and Z06 owners. Contaminated fluid left past the 7,500-mile mark can accelerate clutch pack wear and reduce shift quality.

Recommended fluid: Dexron VI ATF. The dealer will drain, replace the filter cartridge, refill, and perform a relearn procedure via scan tool to reset the clutch adaptation tables.

Battery Systems

12V AGM

Main 12V Battery

The ZR1 uses an AGM battery optimized for the high-current demands of active aero, cooling fans, and electronic systems. If storing the car, use a GM-approved trickle charger to maintain battery health. Standard replacement interval is 4–5 years.

ZR1X Hybrid

High-Voltage Battery Pack

The ZR1X's HV battery requires dealer-level service only. Thermal management is fully automatic. GM recommends keeping the hybrid battery between 20–80% state of charge for long-term storage to maximize pack longevity.
