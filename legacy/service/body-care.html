Cleaning Guide

Paint & Exterior Surfaces

🚿
Two-bucket wash methodSeparate rinse and soap buckets to prevent swirling
🧴
pH-neutral car shampooAvoid dish soap — strips wax and sealant
🪣
Microfiber wash mittSoft-pile for clear-coat safe washing
💧
Rinse top-downStart at roof, finish at rockers — prevents grit dragging
🌬️
Blow-dry or air-dryLeaf blower removes water from body panel seams
🛡️
Ceramic coating recommendedEasier cleaning; protects clear coat long-term

Carbon Fiber Care

🧽
UV-protectant wax or coatingClear carbon weave fades without UV protection
🚫
Avoid abrasive polishesWill scratch the clear coat over the weave
🌡️
Avoid high-pressure near edgesHigh-pressure water can lift carbon panel edges
✅
Carbon-specific sealantMeguiar's Ultimate Black or equivalent

Brake & Wheel Cleaning

Carbon ceramic rotors Rinse only — avoid acidic wheel cleaners
Brake dust Dedicated iron remover spray (pH neutral)
Caliper cleaning Mild soap + soft brush; rinse thoroughly
Forged aluminum wheels pH-neutral wheel soap; seal annually
Lug nuts Torque check after cleaning: 100 lb-ft
Frequency After every track session; monthly street

Storage Preparation

📦
Short-Term (1–3 months)

Short-Term Storage

Full detail and wax. Tire flat-spot prevention by inflating to maximum cold pressure. Battery maintainer connected. Fuel stabilizer added. Climate-controlled garage ideal.

❄️
Long-Term (3–12 months)

Long-Term Storage

Full fluid change before storage. Fuel tank full with stabilizer. Tires on jack stands to prevent flat spots. Battery tender connected. Breathable car cover inside a climate-controlled space.

🏎️
Collector Care

Collector Monthly Checklist

Start and idle to operating temp monthly. Check tire pressure. Inspect seals and weatherstripping. Run A/C to prevent refrigerant seal degradation. Record mileage and condition notes.
