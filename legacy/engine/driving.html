PTM — Performance Traction Management

Launch Control & PTM Pro

PTM is the ZR1's integrated traction, launch, and performance management platform. In launch mode, PTM holds a pre-set RPM, monitors wheel slip on all four corners (ZR1X), and releases clutch engagement at the optimal moment for maximum acceleration without wheelspin.

PTM Pro Mode, exclusive to Track and Z-Mode, allows the driver to dial in exactly how much wheelspin is permitted — from fully locked-down grip to controlled oversteer. It's displayed live on the left instrument cluster screen, showing individual wheel torque and slip percentages.

Push-to-Pass (ZR1X only) is an overboost function that momentarily increases electric motor output for a burst of extra torque beyond the standard calibration.

Performance Data Recorder

The Performance Data Recorder (PDR) records 1080p video from a forward-facing camera while simultaneously logging GPS track position, throttle, brake, steering angle, lateral g-force, speed, and gear selection as a data overlay.

Footage is saved to a microSD card in the center console. The Cosworth-developed software allows post-session analysis on a laptop — replaying the lap with all telemetry channels visible, identifying braking points and cornering lines for improvement.

On ZR1X, the PDR also logs front motor torque output and battery state of charge, providing a complete picture of how the hybrid system was used during each lap.

G-Meter & Telemetry

⏱️
Live Data

G-Meter Display

The ZR1's gauge cluster includes a live g-meter display showing lateral and longitudinal acceleration forces. Combined with the PDR, drivers can analyze how hard they're pushing the chassis relative to its limits.

Analysis

Dyno & Torque Plots

The LT7 torque curve stays essentially flat from 3,000 to 6,750 RPM, over 800 lb-ft, and peaks at 828 lb-ft at 6,000 RPM. Maximum power is 1,064 horsepower at 7,000 RPM. Top speed, 233 mph, is held in sixth gear at an 8,100 RPM redline.

Peak Efficiency on the Road

These cars are built for slow cruising and for full-pace driving. The lower 9.8:1 compression ratio softens the Z06's aggressive feel and reduces the risk of detonation.

Cruise

60 mph in Eighth

You can drive the ZR1 or ZR1X at 60 mph in eighth gear at about 1,400 RPM without the engine bogging down or collecting carbon.

Neutral

Revving in Neutral

With the transmission in neutral, throttle input can raise engine speed while the dual-clutch stays disengaged from the driven wheels. Confirm the car is stationary, keep the brake applied while the state is changing, and return to Park or Drive only when the maneuver is finished.

Ring

Nürburgring Pace

The C8 ZR1 targets a sub-6:50 Nordschleife lap. The ZR1X adds the front e-axle for about 1,250 hp combined and targets the sub-6:50 / sub-6:40 envelope.
