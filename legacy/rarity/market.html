Value Drivers

📦
Production

Low Unit Count

The fewer units built, the higher the long-term value. The C3 ZR1's ~25 units make it one of America's rarest cars.

🏆
Recognition

Awards & Records

Cars that held "fastest in class" or won major awards carry premium valuations — every ZR1 generation qualifies.

📋
Documentation

Original Documentation

Monroney sticker, window sticker, and service history add 10–25% to auction results versus undocumented examples.

🎨
Paint & Options

Original Color & Options

Rare color combinations and factory performance packages (ZTK, Quail, Asymmetrical) command significant premiums.

🛞
Condition

Low Miles / Original

Unrestored, original-paint, low-mileage examples consistently outperform restored or repainted cars at auction.

📈
Timing

Market Timing

ZR1 values tend to dip when new generations debut, then recover as older generations gain collector status — typically within 5–10 years.
