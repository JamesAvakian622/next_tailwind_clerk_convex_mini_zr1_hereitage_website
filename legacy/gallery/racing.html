Corvette Racing

Six decades of factory GT racing — from the C4 GTP prototype through the C8.R and Z06 GT3.R. Chevrolet's racing program has won Le Mans class victories, IMSA championships, and cemented Corvette's place in endurance racing history.

Factory Race Cars

IMSA Prototype

C4 GTP

The C4-based GTP prototype that competed in IMSA's top prototype class in the late 1980s and early 1990s — a direct descendant of the C4 Corvette's platform.

Factory GT

C5-R

The C5-R launched Corvette Racing's modern era, winning its class at Le Mans multiple times in the GT1 category. LS6-powered and built by Pratt & Miller — a landmark in American GT racing.

GT1 / GT2

C6.R

The C6.R competed in both GT1 and GT2/GTLM categories, becoming one of the most successful factory GT racers of its era with multiple Le Mans and ALMS titles over its long career.

GTLM Endurance

C7.R

The C7.R continued Corvette Racing's dominance in IMSA GTLM, winning multiple manufacturers' and drivers' championships. Based on the C7 Stingray with a race-spec LT1 engine variant.

Mid-Engine GTD

C8.R

The mid-engine transition brought a completely new C8.R — now competing in GTD class. The 5.5L flat-plane V8 in race spec form produces a screaming sound note that was immediately iconic.

Customer GT3

Z06 GT3.R

The GT3.R brings Corvette racing to customer teams worldwide — FIA GT3 homologated, available for endurance and sprint racing in IMSA GTD, SRO, and regional GT3 championships globally.
