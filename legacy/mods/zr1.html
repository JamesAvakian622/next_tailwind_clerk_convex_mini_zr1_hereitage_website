ZR1 Modifications 🛠️

Performance tuning, handling, active aero, cooling, electronic displays, battery, and aesthetic modifications for the C8 ZR1 platform.

ZR1X Mods →Stock Engine Tech

Performance Tuning

ECU

ECU Calibration

Third-party calibration via HP Tuners or EFI Live can optimize boost curves, fuel maps, and ignition timing. Note: ECU modifications will void powertrain warranty coverage.

Intake

Intercooler Upgrade

Larger water-to-air intercooler cores reduce charge-air temperatures under sustained boost, particularly valuable during back-to-back track laps where heat soak becomes a factor.

Exhaust

Exhaust Upgrade

Cat-back and axle-back systems from Corsa, Borla, and Kooks optimize exhaust flow while enhancing the LT7's distinct flat-plane soundtrack. Factory active valve integration varies by system.

Handling Mods

Suspension

Coilover / Spring Kits

Aftermarket coilovers or spring kits (e.g., Eibach, H&R) allow ride-height adjustment and spring-rate tuning for specific track configurations. Works alongside MRC dampers.

Wheels

Aftermarket Wheel Sets

Forged monoblock or 3-piece wheels in dedicated track width/offset specs. Center-lock adapter kits allow rapid wheel changes during endurance track days.

⚙️
Alignment

Track Alignment

Negative camber front and rear (−1.5° to −2.5°) improves tire contact patch during cornering. Requires adjustable camber bolts or plates at all four corners.

Active Aero Upgrades

Wing

Wing Extensions

Gurney flaps or swan-neck wing mounts that increase rear downforce while maintaining the factory active wing's range of motion. Compatible with ZTK package geometry.

⬇️
Splitter

Front Splitter Replacement

Carbon fiber splitter extensions and dive planes that work with the factory front aero system. Adds measurable front downforce at track speeds — important for balance with rear wing mods.

Cooling Mods

Intercooler

Upgraded Intercooler Core

Larger tube-and-fin or bar-and-plate cores for the water-to-air intercooler circuit. Reduces heat soak on extended track sessions.

Radiator

Additional Oil Cooler

Sandwich-plate oil cooler adapters for the LT7's dry sump system. Helps maintain oil temperature under extended full-throttle operation.

🌬️
Airflow

Heat Deflectors

Underbody heat deflector panels route hot exhaust air away from the rear suspension and fuel lines — especially useful for cars run without undertray panels.

Electronic Displays

HUD

Aftermarket HUD Systems

OBDII-based HUD projectors and windshield-mounted units that add supplementary data beyond the factory display — boost pressure, oil temp, lap timer, and gear indicator.

📹
Camera

Additional Camera Mounts

Suction-cup and roll bar mount systems for GoPro / Insta360 cameras. Complements the factory PDR with additional angles for post-session video analysis.

Aesthetic & Stripes

Racing stripe packages — dual racing stripes, carbon flash accents, and asymmetrical two-tone configurations. Available as factory options or aftermarket vinyl wraps.
