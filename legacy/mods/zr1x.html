ZR1X-Specific Modifications

Additional modification categories unique to the hybrid E-AWD platform

Hybrid

Battery Management

Third-party battery monitoring systems track state-of-charge cycles and cell balancing. Some tuners offer HV battery recalibration to expand the usable state-of-charge window beyond the conservative factory limits — increasing electric range and regen aggressiveness.

E-AWD

Torque Bias Tuning

The front/rear torque split ratio defaults can be adjusted by authorized calibrators via the ZR1X's torque blending ECU parameters. More front bias improves wet-weather traction; more rear bias sharpens the rear-drive character for track use.

Cooling

HV Thermal Management

The ZR1X's high-voltage battery uses a liquid thermal management system. Upgraded pump flow rates and heat exchanger capacity allow longer sustained full-system output during track days before thermal limiting occurs.

Performance

eMotor Output Calibration

The front electric motor's peak output and duty cycle can be adjusted by authorized ZR1X tuners. Increasing peak torque delivery duration extends the push-to-pass boost effect, while adjusting the regen strength affects brake feel and energy recovery rate.
