Active Aerodynamics

1,000+ lb of Downforce

The C8 ZR1's active aerodynamic system includes a deployable front dive plane, an adjustable rear wing, and underbody elements that work together to generate over 1,000 lb of downforce at speed. In drag-reduction mode, the system flattens out to minimize drag for maximum top speed — allowing the 233 mph run.

The rear wing adjusts automatically based on drive mode. In Track mode, it rises to maximum angle. In high-speed mode, it flattens. The front splitter extends and retracts via actuators controlled by the same system that manages suspension, PTM, and exhaust.

Suspension & Chassis

Suspension

Double-Wishbone

Front and rear double-wishbone suspension with adjustable dampers — the same geometry used in Le Mans-class race cars for precise wheel control.

🧲
Magneto-Rheological

Magnetic Ride Control

MR fluid-filled shock absorbers that can vary their stiffness in milliseconds by applying a magnetic field — the fastest-reacting suspension technology available.

Front Lift

GPS-Based Front Lift

The front lift system raises the nose by 50 mm to clear speed bumps and steep driveways. The GPS memory function automatically lifts when it detects a saved location.

Convertible & Colors

Convertible

Power Retractable Top

The ZR1 Convertible features a power-retractable soft top that operates in under 16 seconds at speeds up to 30 mph. The folded top stores in a dedicated compartment behind the seats.
