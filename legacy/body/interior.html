Performance Seats

Standard

GT1 Seat

The standard GT1 seat balances all-day comfort with lateral support. Leather-wrapped with available Napa upgrades — suitable for daily driving and spirited canyon roads.

Sport

GT2 Seat

Enhanced bolstering with firmer cushioning — the ideal choice for drivers who split time between the track and the street, offering more lateral support under hard cornering.

Track

Competition Seat

Fixed-back carbon fiber bucket — maximum lateral and shoulder support for track days. Requires a neck brace or HANS device for helmet use. Lightest seating option.

Heads-Up Display & Instruments

Full-Color HUD

The ZR1's heads-up display projects a full-color image onto the windshield in the driver's direct line of sight. Content is customizable — speed, RPM, gear, PTM status, navigation, and drive mode can all appear simultaneously.

In Track mode, the HUD switches to a performance layout showing live g-force, PTM Pro slip percentage, and lap timer. The HUD brightness adjusts automatically to ambient light conditions.

Steering Wheel & Controls

Driver Control Center

The ZR1's D-shaped steering wheel is wrapped in microfiber suede (Alcantara) and positions all critical driver controls within thumb reach. Gear shift paddles, PTM mode buttons, drive mode selector, and traction control adjustments are all accessible without leaving the wheel.

The 2026–27 steering wheel adds tactile haptic feedback for mode confirmation — a light pulse when changing drive mode ensures the driver registers the change without looking down from the road.
