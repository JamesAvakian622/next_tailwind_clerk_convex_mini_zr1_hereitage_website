The LT7 engineDifference

The C8 ZR1 marks the first time GM has combined a flat-plane crankshaft V8 with twin turbochargers in a production Corvette. The LT7's flat-plane crank enables a higher revving engine with better exhaust scavenging between cylinders — it screams to 8,400 rpm.

The active aerodynamics system — with a deployable front splitter and adjustable rear wing — enables the car to switch between low-drag (233 mph) and maximum downforce (1,000+ lb) configurations automatically based on driving mode.

Inside the mid-engine layout, the transmission sits behind the engine in a transaxle configuration. The Tremec 8-speed DCT provides lightning-fast gear changes with no torque interruption.
