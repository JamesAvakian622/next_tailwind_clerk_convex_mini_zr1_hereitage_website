The Last Front-Engine ZR1

When Chevrolet unveiled the C7 ZR1 at the 2017 Dubai Motor Show, it represented the pinnacle of seven decades of front-engine Corvette performance. The 2.65-liter supercharger — the largest ever fitted to a Corvette — sits under a special hood with a transparent window revealing the blower.

The ZTK Performance Package added a high-downforce rear wing, Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 tires, and front brake-cooling ducts — producing 950 lb of downforce at top speed while maintaining a 212 mph top speed. The standard low-wing model achieved 115 mph less downforce but identical top-speed capability.

The C7 ZR1 was the swan song of the front-engine era. When the C8 arrived as a mid-engine car, the ZR1 badge moved with it — carrying the legacy forward in an entirely new direction.
