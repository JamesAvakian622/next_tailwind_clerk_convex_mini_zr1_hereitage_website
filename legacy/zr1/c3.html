C3 ZR1 — The Origin

The special performance package that launched the ZR1 legend. The LT1 small-block V8 delivered 370 HP in an era when American muscle ruled the roads.

Overview

The C3 ZR1 was born from Chevrolet's racing program in 1970. The ZR1 package — officially RPO ZR1 — was a special-order solid-lifter performance option that transformed the Corvette into a pure racing machine.

The LT1 engine was derived directly from Zora Arkus-Duntov's racing heritage. With aluminum heads, a solid-lifter camshaft, and an 11:1 compression ratio, it produced a genuine 370 HP at a time when manufacturers were beginning to reduce performance numbers.

Only a handful of C3 ZR1s were ever built, making surviving examples among the rarest and most collectible Corvettes in existence. This is where the ZR1 legend began.
