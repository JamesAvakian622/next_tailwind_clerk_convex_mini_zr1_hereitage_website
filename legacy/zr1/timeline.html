ZR1 Timeline

Six decades of milestones — from the first 370 HP C3 package in 1970 to the 1,250+ HP electrified hypercar arriving in 2026 and beyond.

Milestones

1970
C3 ZR1 Debuts — The Legend Begins
RPO ZR1 option introduced. The solid-lifter LT1 V8 makes 370 HP. Only ~25 units built. Sets the template for all future ZR1s — the highest-performance Corvette option available.
1972
C3 ZR1 Ends Production
The C3 ZR1 package is discontinued after only 3 model years. The ZR1 name goes dormant for 18 years, building mystique and desirability among collectors.
1990
C4 ZR-1 Returns — King of the Hill
The ZR1 badge returns after an 18-year absence with the Lotus-developed LT5 DOHC V8. 375 HP at launch, wider rear body, and a valet key to restrict power. America's fastest production car.
1993
LT5 Upgraded to 405 HP
Revised cylinder heads and camshafts boost the C4 ZR-1 to 405 HP. Sets a world speed record of 175.1 mph at the Kennedy Space Center test track.
1995
C4 ZR-1 Final Year
Production ends after 6 model years and 6,939 total units. The C4 ZR-1 remains one of the most technologically ambitious American cars ever built.
2009
C6 ZR1 — Blue Devil Unleashed
The 638 HP LS9 supercharged ZR1 debuts. Carbon-ceramic brakes, carbon-fiber panels, and a 205 mph top speed. The Blue Devil sets a Nürburgring time of 7:26.4.
2013
C6 ZR1 Final Year
5 model years, ~6,200 units. The C6 ZR1 proved American engineering could compete with — and beat — exotics costing 3-5× as much.
2019
C7 ZR1 — 755 HP Swan Song
The final front-engine ZR1. The LT5 supercharged V8 produces 755 HP. Available with ZTK package delivering 950 lb of downforce and a 212 mph top speed.
2025
C8 ZR1 — Mid-Engine Revolution
The first mid-engine ZR1. The all-new LT7 engineTwin-Turbo flat-plane V8 produces 1,064 HP. Active aerodynamics, Tremec DCT, and a 233 mph top speed establish a new benchmark for American performance.
2026
C8 ZR1X — The Hypercar Arrives
The ZR1X adds E-AWD via a front electric motor to the LT7, creating a 1,250+ HP hybrid hypercar. Stealth Mode, Quail Edition, Asymmetrical Edition, Stars & Steel. Many automotive journalists call it the greatest performance bargain of all time.
2027+
The ZR1X Evolves
Continued development of the ZR1 and ZR1X platforms — updated interior, expanded trim options, and further refinement of the hybrid drivetrain. The ZR1X legacy continues to grow.
