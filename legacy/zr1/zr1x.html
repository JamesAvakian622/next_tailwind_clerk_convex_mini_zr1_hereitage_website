The Hypercar

The ZR1X adds a front-mounted electric motor to the ZR1's LT7 drivetrain, creating a full E-AWD system that distributes torque between the front electric axle and the rear combustion-powered DCT transaxle. The result is traction and launch capability beyond what any purely combustion-powered Corvette could achieve.

Stealth Mode allows the ZR1X to operate on the front electric motor alone — silently navigating parking garages or neighborhoods before unleashing the full 1,250+ HP system. The PTM (Performance Traction Management) system has been recalibrated to account for the instant torque delivery from the eMotor.

Special editions include the Quail ZR1X (revealed at Monterey Car Week), the Asymmetrical Edition with its signature two-tone red-black interior and exterior, and the Stars & Steel Edition limited to 250 units per year.
