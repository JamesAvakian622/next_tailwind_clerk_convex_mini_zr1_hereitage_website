The Blue Devil

The C6 ZR1 debuted at the 2008 Detroit Auto Show and immediately set a new benchmark for American performance. The LS9 engine featured a 2.3-liter Eaton TVS supercharger mounted in the valley of the block, titanium intake valves, and dry-sump lubrication.

Carbon-ceramic brakes were standard — the first Corvette to feature them. The carbon-fiber roof panel, hood, front splitter, and rocker panels reduced weight while increasing rigidity. The suspension was tuned by Lotus for both track and street driving.

The C6 ZR1 set a Nürburgring lap time of 7:26.4 in 2008, making it one of the fastest production cars ever to lap the famous German circuit at that time.
