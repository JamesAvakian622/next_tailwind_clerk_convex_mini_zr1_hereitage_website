C4 ZR-1 — King of the Hill

The Lotus-engineered LT5 DOHC V8 redefined what an American sports car could be. 405 HP, world speed records, and the most technologically advanced Corvette of its era.

The LT5 Engine

The LT5 was a clean-sheet engine developed jointly by Chevrolet and Lotus Engineering. It featured dual overhead camshafts per bank, four valves per cylinder, and a unique two-mode intake system that restricted power in everyday driving until a "Valet Key" was used.

In 1993, the LT5 received a significant upgrade — revised cylinder heads with larger ports, revised camshafts, and new pistons brought output to 405 HP, cementing the C4 ZR-1's status as the fastest production car in America at the time.

The ZF 6-speed manual transmission was standard, and the car's wider rear body — featuring an extra 3 inches of bodywork — distinguished it visually from the base Corvette.
